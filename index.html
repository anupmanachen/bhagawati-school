Welcome to Our School

Shree Bhagawati Secondary School

Established in 2039 B.S. (1982 A.D.), Bhagawati Secondary School stands as a premier educational institution in Shankharapur Municipality, Kathmandu. Designated as an Excellent School among local institutions, we focus on character building, disciplined academic growth, and modern educational methodologies.

  • Affiliated with National Examinations Board (NEB)
  • Well-equipped Science, Computer, and Hotel Management Labs
  • Focus on Extracurriculars, Sports, and Real-World Field Trips

Community Leader

On track to recognition as a Mega School in Shankharapur Municipality.

Academic Excellence

Our Educational Programs

Pre-Primary & Basic Level

Montessori-inspired early learning environment designed to nurture creativity, curiosity, and foundational skills.

Secondary Level (Grades 9 & 10)

Comprehensive standard SEE curriculum enriched with practical science experiments and technology skills.

+2 Computer Science & Mgmt

Equipping students with modern computing, IT fundamentals, and business administrative principles.

+2 Hotel Management

Hands-on hospitality training with dedicated kitchen lab practice and luxury hotel field visits.

+2 Law & Humanities

Preparing the next generation of legal professionals, social thinkers, and administrative leaders.

+2 Education Stream
