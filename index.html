Welcome to Our School

Shree Bhagawati Secondary School

Established in 2039 B.S. (1982 A.D.), Bhagawati Secondary School stands as a premier educational institution in Shankharapur Municipality, Kathmandu.

  • Affiliated with National Examinations Board (NEB)
  • Well-equipped Science, Computer, and Hotel Management Labs
  • Focus on Extracurriculars, Sports, and Field Trips

Community Leader

Serving the Sankhu community with dedication, discipline, and modern infrastructure.
